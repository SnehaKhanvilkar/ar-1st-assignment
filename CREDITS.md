## 3D model
- `assets/heart.glb` is an **original, simplified model generated for this project** (spheres and tubes arranged after your reference heart picture). No third-party 3D model is included, so there is nothing to attribute.
- It is **not medically accurate**. For a more realistic model, replace the file with a GLB whose licence allows your use, and write its creator, source link and licence in this file. Never guess these.

## Images
- `assets/heart-marker.jpg` and `assets/images/heart-illustration.png` are made from the heart illustration **you supplied** (plus a generated border for tracking). I could not check where that picture came from or its licence. Make sure you are allowed to use and print it, or swap in an image you own (README, "Use your own marker").
- The UI layout was designed from your reference poster. No part of the poster is used as an image.
